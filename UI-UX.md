### Matching Rules — Latest Documented Decision

Use strict exact matching as the current business rule.

Example:

Left Side = ৳500,000  
Right Side = ৳300,000

Result:
- No matching.
- No team matching commission.
- Both balances remain pending / carried forward.

If both sides subsequently meet an eligible identical slot amount, that exact slot can be matched according to the approved matching configuration.

Important:
- No automatic partial matching.
- No automatic smaller-slot cascading.
- No flushing of unmatched amounts.
- Preserve unmatched balances for future matching.

The handling of excess volume, repeated matches, and partial residual amounts must remain configurable until the client confirms the final edge-case rules.

Create a clear visual comparison between Left and Right balances, including matching eligibility indicators.
